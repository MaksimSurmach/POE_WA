## Price and publication boundary

New recipes use the existing durable collector, source access configuration, pacing and immutable snapshots. Input acquisition is exact level; output comparison allows higher item levels. Historical prices are not imported. Missing quote/property/material stock evidence withholds economics. A missing Serrated price must not be replaced with a Chaos price: the five selected Chaos procedures have their own verified probabilities and costs, with the original fossil procedures retained as comparisons.

Expanded full refreshes may exceed the former one-hour queue deadline. The queue is bounded at two hours, with a105-minute transient admission budget and15-minute completion headroom; queued-wait expiry remains separate. Existing worker/scheduler supervision and single flight remain in use.
