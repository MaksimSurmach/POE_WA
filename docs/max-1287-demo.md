## Two real research profiles

- **Flicker Strike Slayer, Stygian Vise, Allflame 3.29**: [guide](https://odealo.com/articles/trinity-flicker-strike-slayer-build-1) specifies at least 100 life, 80 total elemental resistance and 30% elemental damage with attacks. Item level, influence and corruption requirements are not stated. On 2026-10-29 at 20:34:59 UTC, an Instant Buyout search showed five selected listings at 1 divine each. Two cheaper matches in chaos were not mixed with these. [Manual observation](evidence/max-1287-flicker-trade-2026-10-29.json) records the exact selected rows, search, seller, visible stats, gold fee and relative listing age. Prices are asks, not sales.
- **Winter Orb Elementalist, Stygian Vise, Allflame 3.29**: [PoB](https://pobb.in/W4yCI3RRdniV) describes 110+ life, 26–30% chaos resistance from Essence of Envy and additional resistances without a numeric threshold. Five selected Instant Buyout listings with life >=110, chaos resistance >=26 and at least one other resistance were visible at 60–120 chaos on 2026-10-29 at 20:33:56 UTC. [Manual observation](evidence/max-1287-winter-orb-trade-2026-10-29.json) contains the selected raw transcriptions and scope.

These are manually selected snapshots, not market-wide representative samples. Trade listing age is not sale velocity, and Instant Buyout may charge a gold fee. The records deliberately do not infer influence, corruption or missing resistances. They are not proof of full comparability: executable target rules and the remaining build requirements have not been verified. The application reports `PARTIAL` and `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` rather than ranking these offers.

The Winter Orb method uses the Allflame mechanic and instructs replacement of a base after excess Intangibility. Success probability and replacement cost are unknown; the current economics engine assumes repeated attempts on a reusable base, so it must not calculate this method. The Flicker guide describes desired gear but does not establish a complete reproducible crafting method or probability. No real expected craft cost or potential spread is asserted for either profile.

To load these dated observations into the configured PostgreSQL database without modifying existing records:

```sh
pnpm market:seed-library
pnpm market:import-belt-evidence
```

The second command is idempotent for the same target and observation. These observations are historical after their capture time and the freshness gate will eventually prevent valuation. They are never merged with demo data.

## Product workflow

The target form accepts explicit modifier thresholds, optional total elemental resistance, method steps, a probability or expected attempts, and dated manual base/material prices without JSON. Matching v3 lets a user deliberately leave item level, corruption and influence unrestricted for finished listings; when a restriction is set, missing evidence remains unknown. Its optional resistance threshold still requires all three observed per-element values. Versions 1 and 2 retain their original behavior. Unrestricted listing filters do not prove a crafting base is suitable: the method, base and all costs still require separate evidence. The form supports methods only when the operator confirms that all attempts use the same base with no replacement, recovery or finishing costs. Other methods remain uncalculated.

The snapshot form accepts individual user-entered listings and optional original text without JSON. Raw saved item payloads remain unchanged. Each snapshot uses one currency; there is no invented exchange rate. The analysis records per-row reasons, excludes nonmatches and deduplicates sellers. A fresh sample requires at least five confirmed independent sellers; the conservative asking estimate uses the cheapest confirmed offer. User confirmations and manual prices are assertions, not automatic source verification.

New price observations are stored separately from immutable target profiles, with source, time, league, patch, currency and data kind. Their history is visible in the price dialog. The latest matching-kind update is used, with no fallback for missing material prices; stale or incompatible prices block calculation. A valid calculation is `CALCULATED` with `POSITIVE` or `NON_POSITIVE` as a separate economic conclusion. No calculation is a guarantee of a sale.

## Reproducible checks

The separate `pnpm market:seed-belt-demo` remains a synthetic test, explicitly marked DEMO. Its inputs are 10 chaos base, 2 chaos per attempt and a synthetic 25% chance, giving 10 + 4 × 2 = 18 chaos expected cost; the selected cheapest asking price is 40 chaos, yielding an illustrative 22 chaos difference. Updating the synthetic base to 40 gives 40 + 4 × 2 = 48 chaos and a negative 8 chaos difference. These are regression checks only, not real market or probability evidence.

Local checks on Node 24: workspace unit suite, typecheck, lint and build; isolated PostgreSQL 17 integration and migration checks; UI forms, manual demo import and price history, and API restart. Refer to PR CI for supported Node 22.

## Follow-up: concrete item and a candidate recipe

The [Winter Orb PoB](https://pobb.in/W4yCI3RRdniV) contains an equipped rare Stygian Vise, item level 80, with **139 life, 38% fire resistance and 29% chaos resistance**, plus energy shield, mana and flask charges. Its [extracted item text](evidence/max-1287-winter-orb-pob-belt-2026-10-29.json) comes from item 12 in the publicly displayed build code, and the Belt slot references that item. These are the example item's actual values, not minimum requirements. The guide explicitly says additional resistances depend on what the character is missing and does not specify a numerical belt threshold.

For Flicker, a **candidate**, not a recipe supplied by the guide, is to start with an uncorrupted, non-influenced, unfractured normal Stygian Vise, apply an Orb of Alchemy, and repeatedly use Harvest's Attack reforge on the resulting rare item until it has at least 100 life, 80 total elemental resistance and 30% increased elemental attack damage, with no further finishing step. The [Harvest recipe](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Harvest_crafting) costs **75 Wild Crystallised Lifeforce per reforge** and guarantees an Attack modifier; the [modifier-pool description](https://www.exiledbench.com/blog/harvest-crafting-explained) identifies elemental attack damage as the natural Attack family on an ordinary uninfluenced Stygian Vise. The guaranteed family does not guarantee the required roll, life or resistances. The proposed route reuses the base and requires no Allflame replacement, but its complete hit probability and applicability to the observed base offers have not been independently verified. It is not a calculated opportunity.

At 2026-10-29 22:24:11 UTC, the [poe.ninja currency overview](https://poe.ninja/poe1/api/economy/exchange/current/overview?league=Allflame&type=Currency) reported **0.01734 chaos per Wild Lifeforce**, **0.01993 chaos per Orb of Alchemy** and **0.009865 chaos per Dead Man's Sulphur**; the [raw response](evidence/max-1287-ninja-currency-2026-10-29.json) is retained. At 22:24:17 UTC the [Essence overview](https://poe.ninja/poe1/api/economy/exchange/current/overview?league=Allflame&type=Essence) reported **3.33 chaos per Shrieking Essence of Envy**; its [raw response](evidence/max-1287-ninja-essence-2026-10-29.json) is retained. These are aggregated economy estimates, not individual seller asks or completed sales.

An independent arithmetic check gives **75 × 0.01734 = 1.3005 chaos per Flicker reforge**, excluding the base and initial Alchemy. The Winter Orb essence alone is **3.33 chaos per attempt**; sulphur adds **0.009865 × quantity** chaos at the sampled rate. The quantity, success probabilities and expected attempt counts are not established, so neither figure is the cost per successful belt. The historical 3/5-chaos base asks above remain unverified for these method-specific constraints and must be refreshed before use.

The [developer interview](https://dujseks5cqq0r.cloudfront.net/mmo/news-easier-exist-redo-path-exile-s-mark-octavian-talk-allflame-league-mercenaries-reworks) describes Intangibility as the percentage chance of an Allflame craft failing to provide its multiple outcomes. The [league guide](https://dev.poe-vault.com/guides/curse-of-the-allflame-mechanic-guide) describes 2–4 ghostly copies depending on method and variable Intangibility gains. [Craft of Exile's changelog](https://beta.craftofexile.com/changelog?game=poe1) says its beta added Allflame simulation, sulphur costs and Intangibility editing. These sources do not establish a validated full Shrieking Envy sequence for this belt, its exact sulphur consumption, gain distribution or replacement frequency. The separate Aquapado simulator explicitly labels those numbers placeholders and is not a probability source.

## Winter Orb resistance threshold investigation

The same PoB export also records player fire resistance **76% with 1% over cap** and chaos resistance **75% with 2% over cap**. It records cold **76% with 4% over cap** and lightning **76% with 18% over cap**. These raw `Build/PlayerStat` values are now in the [item evidence](evidence/max-1287-winter-orb-pob-belt-2026-10-29.json). PoB's [resistance calculation](https://github.com/PathOfBuildingCommunity/PathOfBuilding/blob/dev/src/Modules/CalcDefence.lua) defines over-cap as the positive difference between total and maximum resistance.

For this exact saved configuration, the estimated replacement thresholds to retain the same caps are **37% fire** (38 − 1) and **27% chaos** (29 − 2). On 2026-10-29, the original build was opened in [PoB for the web](https://pob.cool/#build=https://pobb.in/W4yCI3RRdniV), version 2.67.2, tree 3.29. A controlled replacement retaining the original belt's other modifiers and Abyss jewel, but with **37% fire and 27% chaos**, produced **76/76/76/75** fire/cold/lightning/chaos resistance. Replacing it with **36% fire and 26% chaos** produced **75/76/76/74**. The two controlled inputs and observed results are recorded in the [evidence](evidence/max-1287-winter-orb-pob-belt-2026-10-29.json). These are test items, not market observations. The check establishes the resistance boundary only for this saved configuration with the remaining belt properties unchanged; it does not prove that an item with merely the guide's **110+ life** and these resistances preserves the build's other properties. The guide does not publish these as universal minimums.

None of the five historical Winter Orb offers recorded above has a fire-resistance modifier in its transcribed affixes. They therefore do not satisfy the verified 37% fire condition as recorded. A resistance swap, if applicable, would be a separate finishing operation with its own cost and result, not evidence that the listed item already matches.

In Craft of Exile beta's simulation route editor, `PSEUDO Flat Life` and `PSEUDO Elemental Resists` accept numeric values, while the displayed individual modifier requirement uses tiers; the displayed requirement list did not expose an Intangibility condition. A Shrieking Envy step was configured, but a complete, independently validated simulation enforcing 110 life, the individual resistance rolls and replacement at the guide's threshold was not obtained. No success probability or expected cost is asserted from this partial setup.
