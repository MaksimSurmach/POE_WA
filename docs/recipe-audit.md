- **Shield:** The active ilvl 60 shield receives its ≥130 life from Deafening Essence of Greed, which specifies 130–144 life for shields. - **Belts:** Both ilvl 85 Stygian models explicitly select life tier 1 (130–144) in their stored calculator settings. The [modifier list](https://poedb.tw/us/Modifiers_list) lists belt life 130–144 at level 64; ilvl 85 admits it. Live CoE links reproduced 0.163% / 613 Envy and 0.106% / 942 fossil sets. - **Bench:** Stored fire resistance costs 3 Chaos on the bone helmet and shield and Trigger costs 3 Chaos on the wand; the [bench listing](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Crafting_bench) corroborates these. A Scouring on the wand is described as optional reserve but charged as a material: review whether cost is expected expenditure or a budget reserve.
- **Enkindling:** Both flask steps use p=0.034965034965, or 3.4965% per orb. [Modifier list](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/List_of_enkindling_orb_flask_enchantment_modifiers) lists five Enkindling modifiers with weights 100, 100, 100, 250, 100. - **Starting rarity:** Use `startingBase` for acquisition. The physical Alteration recipe buys normal, transmutes once, then uses Alterations; its separate `base` is magic calculator state. The Jagged recipe also acquires normal. Confirm provider queries respect these distinctions.
- **Physical Chaos calculator:** Current CoE readback of the stored link returned 0.00869%, 1 in 11,502, and 11,502 Chaos for ilvl 83 and all three named notables. The calculator base displays 8–12 passives; equivalence to the exact 8-passive Trade target remains an assumption.
